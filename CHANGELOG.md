# Changelog

## v1.3 — Two Roads (Nov 23, 2026)
- **You are still the owner / manager, never the driver.**
- **Careers start in karting:** a cheap kart team ($6K as an owner, or a kart team hires you) in a regional kart series at real US kart tracks: Orlando Kart Center (the season opener), Ocala Gran Prix, GoPro Motorplex, New Castle Motorsports Park, the Charlotte Motor Speedway kart track and PittRace. Karts have their own pixel art, 20-lap sprints, no stages and young drivers (ages 12+). Kart layouts are simplified pixel versions, not surveyed maps.
- **Pick your path after the kart season:** NASCAR (ARCA > Trucks > O'Reilly > Cup) or the Road to IndyCar (USF Juniors > USF2000 > USF Pro 2000 > Indy NXT > IndyCar). Owners sell the kart and local backers top the bank up to at least $15K for the first big car; kart R&D gives that car a small edge. Hired managers get one NASCAR offer and one Indy offer. You can also run another kart season.
- **Road to IndyCar:** open-wheel cars, IndyCar-style points (+1 pole, +1 led a lap, +2 most laps led), no stages, and real-style schedules on 15 new real tracks (St. Petersburg and Long Beach streets, Barber, the Indianapolis road course, Detroit, Road America, Mid-Ohio, Laguna Seca, Toronto, Portland, NOLA, Lucas Oil, Gateway / WWT Raceway, Milwaukee, Nashville) plus Iowa, Watkins Glen, Sonoma and the Indianapolis oval. IndyCar opens at St. Pete, runs the Indy 500 in race 5 (longer race, bonus purse, its own trophy) and ends at Nashville with double points.
- **Free Mode:** a FREE MODE button on the hub (Q on a keyboard). Pick any series, any track (the series' own or all 45) and any team, then race a one-off weekend. Works without a career and never touches the career save; leaving at any point puts your career back exactly as it was.
- **Tracks look more like the real thing:** grandstands where the real ones are (Daytona's huge frontstretch, Bristol's bowl all the way around, Martinsville's front and back stands, Talladega's long frontstretch, Indy's turn stands), infield garages, Daytona's and Talladega's infield lakes, and city blocks with concrete walls on street courses.
- **Real pit roads:** cars peel off before the start/finish at a yellow commitment line, every car has its own evenly spaced pit box along a long pit road (on short tracks it wraps into the turns, like Bristol and Martinsville; the pole sitter gets the box nearest pit exit), stopped cars pull into their box, and the exit blends back in after turn 1-2.
- **Merch and team events:** merch sales every race (rep, results and the new Fan Shop), and random team events (about 30% of races): sponsor bonus checks, viral moments, fan crowdfunds, local TV features, contingency awards, hauler breakdowns, series fines, shop floods, tire price hikes, an engineer quitting, a driver with the flu. Bad events are rarer while you are in the red.
- **Tech inspection:** qualifying trim, a car far above the field and brand-new R&D parts raise the risk (shown on the practice setup buttons). Failures cost a fine, a fine plus 10-25 points, or (rarely) a disqualification to last place.
- **Playoffs with eliminations:** Cup 16 (after R4) > 12 (R6) > 8 (R8) > Championship 4 (R9) with win-and-advance; O'Reilly 12 > 8 > 4 and Trucks 10 > 8 > 4. IndyCar and Indy NXT end with a double-points finale. A save made mid-season before v1.3 finishes that season on the old format.
- **A car for every series:** kart, ARCA, Truck, O'Reilly, Cup, USF (junior open-wheel), USF Pro, Indy NXT and IndyCar (with aeroscreen), in the race view, garage, podium and menus.
- **Contract extensions:** offer +1 to +3 years as a Lowball, Fair or Generous deal (max 5 years). Morale, age and team rep decide if they sign; a yes lifts morale, a no lowers it and you wait a race to ask again. Drivers in their final year are flagged.
- **More upgrades:** facilities go to level 8; new Wind Tunnel (aero), Chassis Rig (chassis) and Fan Shop (merch); R&D gets Major Project (Shop LV 3) and Moonshot (Shop LV 6) tiers.
- **Names:** a new game starts with EMPTY owner and team name boxes (placeholder text only) and you must type both. A bad-word blocker checks every name you type: owner name (new game, hired manager and profile edits), team name and driver names. You can now rename your drivers. Generated names are untouched.
- **ARCA is a little friendlier:** slightly weaker top teams, bigger purses, cheaper crash repairs, top 6 (or 2 wins) earns the call-up, and the Trucks buy-in drops to $150K. In bot tests most teams now move up after their 2nd ARCA season instead of the 3rd or 4th.
- **Saves:** v1.0, v1.1, v1.2 and v1.2.1 saves all load and stay on the NASCAR path; they get the new facilities at level 1. Players with a save see a "what's new" pop-up once.

## v1.2.1 — PC support (Nov 23, 2026)
- **PC support: better scaling, mouse and keyboard.**
- **Scaling:** on a computer the game is laid out at a phone-like size and scaled up to fit the window, centred, with a sensible shape (letterboxed on 4:3 and ultra-wide screens). No giant stretched tiles, no tiny text. Checked at 1280x720, 1366x768, 1920x1080 and 1024x768.
- **Sharp pixel font:** the pixel font now lands on whole screen pixels at any display scaling (it used to look uneven on 1x desktop screens). The race view and podium keep the same chunky pixels as on a phone.
- **Mouse:** hover highlights on tiles, buttons, cards and list rows, a pointer cursor, and click works everywhere tap does. The mouse wheel scrolls lists, and turns into sideways scrolling on card rows (like sponsor offers) once the page can't scroll any further. Trophy shelves wrap onto extra rows on desktop so every trophy shows. Thin scrollbars show on desktop.
- **Keyboard:** Esc = back / close, Enter or Space = Go Race / confirm. Race day: 1 Push, 2 Normal, 3 Save, 4 or P Pit Now, Up/Down arrows change pace, T (or Left/Right) switches car, C camera, F speed. Pit menus: 1-4 to pick, Enter = the crew chief's call, Esc = cancel. Small key hints show on computers only (mouse + keyboard), never on phones.
- **No rotate-your-phone prompt on computers**, even in a tall, narrow window.
- **Phones unchanged:** 844x390 and 740x360 landscape play exactly as in v1.2. v1.0, v1.1 and v1.2 saves all load. The update pop-up only appears for new feature versions, so this patch just lights the NEW dot on the version label.

## v1.2 — Pit Wall (Nov 22, 2026)
- **New Pit Wall look:** the whole game moves to a dark pit-wall theme with charcoal panels, checkered-flag trim on every header, and neon yellow and red accents. Garage, Drivers, Facilities, Sponsors, Calendar, Standings, Trophies, Profile, News, the Update Log and race-day menus all match.
- **New main hub, every tile on one screen:** Go Race, Garage, Drivers, Facilities, Sponsors, Calendar, Standings, Trophies and a big Career tile with a mini Road-to-the-Cup bar (ARCA > TRUCKS > O'REILLY > CUP, with a YOU marker). No sideways scrolling on 844x390 or 740x360 phones. The top bar shows the team name, car, week/day and money.
- **Career dashboard ("Pit Wall Dashboard"):** opens from the Career tile. It has a pixel portrait of your driver drawn from their data (skin, hair and a firesuit in team colours) with Speed, Racecraft and Tires bars (tap for the full card), a car toggle for two-car teams, the season calendar strip with track icons, a big NEXT race panel, mini standings with your team highlighted (shown even outside the top 5), sponsor slots ("EMPTY - GET A TOP 10" until you earn one), the career path (owner promotion requirements, or the hired-manager boss confidence meter and job offers), big Garage, Drivers, Sponsors and Trophies buttons, and a glowing GO RACE. Career history and "start new career" are behind the HISTORY button.
- **News ticker:** a TV-style crawl at the bottom of the hub, driven by real game news (next race, sponsor offers, results, standings, scouting reports, R&D and the news feed). Tap it for the full news.
- **Real tracks, starting with Daytona:** 24 real tracks (names only, no logos): Daytona, Talladega, Atlanta, Charlotte, Las Vegas, Kansas, Texas, Homestead-Miami, Michigan, Pocono, Darlington, Indianapolis, Dover, Phoenix, Richmond, Bristol, Martinsville, Iowa, Elko, Toledo, Berlin, Salem, Watkins Glen and Sonoma. Layouts are roughly the real shapes (tri-ovals, quad-ovals, Michigan's D, Pocono's triangle, Darlington's egg, Phoenix's dogleg, the Martinsville paperclip, the Indy rectangle, two road courses). Each track's real length and banking set its lap time, drafting, side-by-side racing and tire wear.
- **Real schedules:** every series runs 10 real tracks it races at. ARCA and Cup both open at Daytona. The Cup season opens with the Daytona 500 (with a bonus purse) and ends with the championship race at Phoenix. Trucks and O'Reilly also end at Phoenix.
- **Restyled screens:** the career-mode pick (Team Owner / Hired Manager cards), the Team Owner setup (one-screen form with a body/stripe paint toggle), and season end (result, prize and bank, the champion, season highlights, a race-by-race strip, trophies won this season, and your promotion or job choice).
- **Saves:** v1.0 and v1.1 saves load as they are. Old made-up tracks map to the closest real track (Bayshore > Daytona, Talla Pines > Talladega, Thunder Valley > Bristol, Paperclip > Martinsville, Hickory Bowl > Richmond, Riverbend > Iowa, Lone Star > Texas, Carolina > Charlotte, Desert Sun > Las Vegas, Heartland > Kansas, Blue Ridge > Watkins Glen, Lakeside > Sonoma) in the schedule, results, trophies and news.

## v1.1 — Two Cars & Talent (Nov 22, 2026)
- **Second car:** buy a 2nd chassis in the Garage once the team has 20+ reputation and the cash ($45K in ARCA, more in higher series). It has its own driver, its own primary sponsor, its own prize money, and per-race travel & crew costs (about $6K/race in ARCA) plus driver pay and repairs. You can sell it back for 40%.
- **Both team cars race:** both are highlighted on track (yellow arrow = car 1, cyan = car 2) and in the leaderboard. Each car gets its own pit-wall calls (tap the car tabs on race day), and under caution you're asked for each car in turn.
- **Owner points:** Standings has a new Owners tab that adds both cars together.
- **Hired Manager:** bigger teams in Trucks and up can run two cars.
- **Driver stats:** Speed, Consistency, Racecraft, Tire Mgmt, Fuel Saving, Pit Road/Restarts and Aggression, all used by the race sim. Drivers also show Age, Potential stars, Morale, Salary and Contract on pixel stat cards. Young drivers grow toward their potential; veterans slow down.
- **Scouting:** send a Local, Regional or National scout to Karting, Late Models, Dirt Ovals or Legends Cars. Prospects show up as stat ranges and potential-star ranges that narrow each time you scout them again. A higher Simulator level sharpens reports. Prospects sign with other teams if you wait too long.
- **Development drivers:** sign up to 2 prospects to grow on the side, then swap them into a car.
- **Podium celebration:** after every race, the top 3 stand on an animated pixel podium with confetti, champagne and a trophy, plus extra fireworks when your car is up there. A bigger night-sky version crowns the season champion. Tap anywhere or press Results to skip.
- **Trophy cabinet:** a new Trophies tile on the hub with shelves for race wins (per track), podiums (per track), championships (per series), Cup playoffs (berth, Championship 4, Cup champion), promotions, and milestones (first start, first top-10, first win, pole, stage win, first sponsor, first prospect, two-car team, one-two finish, 10 and 25 wins, millionaire). Locked trophies show as silhouettes with how to earn them. Tap any trophy for details.
- **Update log:** tap the V1.1 label on the hub (or Profile > Update Log). "What's new" shows once for existing players.
- **Saves:** v1.0 saves load automatically. Every driver gets the new stats, and trophies are rebuilt from the current season's results and your season history.

## v1.0 — Initial release (Nov 22, 2026)
- Draftmaster-style pixel hub: Team, Garage, Drivers, Facilities, Sponsors, Calendar, Standings.
- Two careers: Team Owner (grassroots $15K start, used car lot, rookies, sponsors earned by results) or Hired Manager.
- The ladder: ARCA > Trucks > O'Reilly > Cup, with Cup playoffs and a Championship 4 finale.
- Race sim with drafting, passing, tire wear, fuel, cautions, stages and pit stops on 12 tracks.
- Pit-wall calls: Push / Normal / Save, and 4 tires / 2 tires / fuel / stay out under caution.
- Autosave plus export/import save codes.
