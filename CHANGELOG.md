# Stock Car Manager - Update Log

## v1.1 (coming soon, in testing)
- Second car: earn and run a two-car team, with its own driver, pit calls and sponsor
- Driver stats: Speed, Consistency, Racecraft, Tire Management, Fuel Saving, Pit Road/Restarts, Aggression, plus Age, Potential, Morale and Contract. Stats change how races play out
- Driver scouting: pay scouts to find prospects from feeder series; the more you scout, the more you learn, including hidden Potential
- Podium celebration: the top three finishers on the podium with confetti after every race, and a bigger one for season champions
- Trophy cabinet: a new Trophies room on the hub for race wins, podiums, championships and milestones
- In-game update log
- Old saves carry over

## v1.0 - November 22, 2026
The first full version. You're the team owner, not the driver.
- Draftmaster-style pixel-art hub: Go Race, Garage, Drivers, Facilities, Sponsors, Calendar, Career Mode, Standings
- Two careers: Team Owner and Hired Manager
- Climb the ladder: ARCA, Trucks, O'Reilly, Cup, with a Cup playoff
- Grassroots start: $15K, a used car lot, rookie drivers, and sponsors you earn with results
- Top-down race view with cars drafting in packs
- Pit-wall calls: Push, Normal, Save, Pit Now, and caution calls with a crew chief tip
- Race speeds: 1x, 4x, Skip
- Autosave, plus export and import of your save
